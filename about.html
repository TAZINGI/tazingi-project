About EduCanada Scholarship Programs

Learn about our mission, eligibility criteria, and how we empower international students from Africa and around the globe.

Our Mission

Bridging Nations Through Academic Excellence

The EduCanada Scholarship Portal connects exceptional international candidates—particularly from across Africa, Asia, and Latin America—with funding opportunities provided by top Canadian universities.

Whether you are pursuing Undergraduate studies, Master's degree research, or Doctoral fellowships, our comprehensive packages help turn your ambition into real-world achievement.

What Our Scholarships Cover

Our comprehensive financial support packages are tailored for full peace of mind.

100% Tuition Fees

Full coverage of university tuition for the entire duration of your program.

Housing Allowance

Monthly stipends to assist with campus accommodation or off-campus living expenses.

Health Insurance

Comprehensive medical and health insurance throughout your study period in Canada.

Travel Grants

Annual travel subsidies for international relocation and academic conferences.
